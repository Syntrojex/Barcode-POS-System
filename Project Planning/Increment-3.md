# Increment 3 — Inventory, Discounts, Reporting & Advanced Billing

## Overview

Increment 3 adds product categories, inventory management, seasonal and special discounts, detailed billing, PDF bill printing, and management reports to the Point of Sale (PoS) system.

Managers can configure product categories, generate category-based barcodes, add and edit products, manage inventory, set discount seasons, and generate reports. Cashiers can record, hold, resume, and edit orders with a minimum number of clicks. Both cashiers and managers can print bills as PDF files.

## Objectives

- Introduce product categories and category-based barcode generation.
- Allow managers to add and edit products through the GUI.
- Allow managers to view and update inventory.
- Keep the available quantity of every product up to date with each order.
- Allow existing orders to be opened and edited under controlled rules.
- Store detailed bill information in `Bill.txt` and `BillDetail.txt`.
- Support seasonal discounts and special discounts.
- Print bills as PDF.
- Provide tabular management reports.
- Provide separate cashier and manager features in the user interface.
- Keep order recording fast, with a minimum number of clicks and button presses.

## Functional Requirements

### A. Product Categories & Barcode Generation

1. The manager shall be able to configure product categories through the GUI before generating barcodes.
2. A file `PCategory.txt` shall be maintained. Each line of the file, ending with `#`, is one product category.
3. The first two digits of a product barcode shall represent the product category (the Category ID).
4. A manager shall be able to generate a barcode for a product and add new products to `Product.txt`.
5. The Category ID shall be stored in `Product.txt` against each product.
6. A manager shall be able to add or edit any product.

### B. Orders & Inventory

7. An existing order can be opened and edited by a cashier.
8. An order with `paid` status can be edited within 7 days of the order payment, and only after approval by a manager. This is different from the handling of orders that are `on-hold`.
9. A manager can see the inventory of the products at any time, as a list of products with their available quantities.
10. A manager can update the inventory.
11. The available quantity of products shall be updated with every order.

### C. Billing, Discounts & Printing

12. A bill can be printed as a PDF by a cashier or a manager.
13. `Bill.txt` stores one bill per line (see Data File Specifications).
14. `BillDetail.txt` stores the bill lines showing the product information included in each bill.
15. The manager shall set seasons for special discounts by saving the start date, end date, and discount percentage of each season in `Seasons.txt`.
16. A manager can add new discounts through the user interface and set a discount active or inactive in `Seasons.txt`.
17. When a bill is generated, the system shall calculate the final amount based on the discount percentage if the current date (the date on which the bill is made) is within the dates mentioned in `Seasons.txt`. The bill shall mention this amount in the seasonal discount field.
18. A special discount, not more than 8 percent, can be applied to a bill.

### D. Reports

19. The manager shall be able to generate the reports listed in the Reports section, in tabular form.

### E. Usability & Roles

20. Recording an order shall need a minimum number of clicks and button presses. Extra clicks or button presses while scanning a product barcode are not desired.
21. Putting an order on hold shall be easy and shall need a minimum number of clicks and button presses.
22. An on-hold order shall be resumable with minimum user interaction.
23. The application shall provide features to the cashier and to the manager separately through the user interface.

## Reports

All reports are generated by the manager and shown in tabular form.

| # | Report | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incomplete orders | All orders that are not `paid` or `archived`, with the total amount of all such bills |
| 2 | Completed orders | All orders that have been completed, with the total amount of these bills |
| 3 | Cash sales | All cash sales for a selected period: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or a specified time period |
| 4 | All sales | All sales with the total amount and the discounts |
| 5 | Top ten most sold items | The ten most sold items in descending order, with their remaining stock |
| 6 | Top ten items by remaining stock | The ten items with the highest remaining quantity, in descending order, so that their purchase is not initiated |

## Data File Specifications

Each record occupies one line. Fields are space-separated.

### PCategory.txt

Each category occupies one line ending with `#`. Fields:

1. Category ID (2 digits; used when generating barcodes)
2. Category name
3. Category acronym

Example line:

```text
10 Beverages BEV #
```

### Product.txt

The Category ID of the product is stored in `Product.txt` against each product.

### Bill.txt

Each bill occupies one line. Fields, in this order:

1. Bill ID (9 digits, unique, cannot repeat in the file)
2. Customer name
3. Order ID (from `Order.txt`)
4. Total bill without tax
5. Total tax
6. Total applicable discount (the sum of the discounts on all bought products)
7. Total payable bill
8. Amount received
9. Balance returned
10. Bill payment status (the order status from `Order.txt`)
11. Seasonal discount
12. Special discount (not more than 8 percent)
13. Bill date
14. Bill time

### BillDetail.txt

Each record is one bill line showing a product included in a bill. Fields, in this order:

1. Bill ID (9 digits, from `Bill.txt`)
2. Product acronym
3. Product unit price without tax
4. Product quantity
5. Discount on the bought product (for example, if 10 products are bought with a discount of 5 rupees on each, this field stores 50)

### Seasons.txt

Each season occupies one line ending with `#`. Fields, in this order:

1. Start date
2. End date
3. Discount percentage
4. isActive (`true` or `false`)

## Business Rules

- The first two digits of every barcode are the Category ID of the product.
- A `paid` order can be edited only within 7 days of its payment and only after manager approval.
- The available quantity of a product is updated with every order.
- A Bill ID is a unique 9-digit number and never repeats in `Bill.txt`.
- The seasonal discount is applied when the bill date lies within the start and end dates of an active season in `Seasons.txt`.
- The special discount cannot exceed 8 percent.
- The discount stored in `BillDetail.txt` is the total discount for the product line (unit discount multiplied by quantity).

## Software Engineering Principles

The implementation should emphasize:

- **Single Responsibility Principle (SRP):** Each class and module should have one clear responsibility, for example category handling, inventory, discount calculation, and report generation as separate components.
- **Open/Closed Principle (OCP):** Components should support extension, such as new reports or new discount types, without unnecessary modification of existing functionality.
- **Code Quality:** Keep code readable, consistent, and maintainable.
- **Reusability:** Design independent modules with clear interfaces.
- **Testing:** Every module includes basic tests, and `CMakeLists.txt` is updated whenever new source files are added.
